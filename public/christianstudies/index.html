A Message from Our Dean

Phillip L. Pointer, D.Min.

"The mission of the James & Rosa McKissic School of Christian Studies is more than to offer a program — it is a sacred trust."

The James & Rosa McKissic School of Christian Studies at Arkansas Baptist College is a faith-grounded, HBCU-rooted academic initiative committed to equipping the next generation of church leaders, educators, counselors, and ministry administrators for the glory of God and the good of the world.

Whether you are called to preach, teach, counsel, administer, lead worship, or serve your community, there is a path here for you. Every course, every conversation, and every practicum hour is designed to form not just your mind — but your character and your calling.

“We are not just awarding degrees. We are releasing ministers, counselors, educators, and leaders into the world — equipped by faith, formed by scholarship, and sent with purpose.”

— Dr. Phillip L. Pointer, Dean of the McKissic School

PROGRAMS

Degree Offerings

You don't just enroll in a program — you enroll in a calling. Five distinct concentrations, one foundation of faith and scholarship.

Christian Education

Teach the Word

Curriculum design, teaching methodology, and educational leadership for youth, church, and denominational contexts.

  • Ministry of Education track
  • Children's & Youth Ministry
  • Christian school leadership

Church Administration

Lead Well. Steward Faithfully.

Organizational leadership, strategic planning, and resource stewardship for growing churches and faith-based organizations.

  • Church & nonprofit management
  • Strategic planning & budgeting
  • Human resource leadership

Worship Arts

Where Excellence Meets Purpose

Worship theology, sacred music history, and liturgical leadership — rooted in the HBCU worship tradition.

  • Worship leadership
  • Sacred music history
  • Liturgical arts coordination

Homiletics & Pastoral Leadership

Preach with Power

The craft of biblical preaching and pastoral care, grounded in the African American preaching tradition.

  • Expository preaching
  • Pastoral theology
  • AA preaching tradition

Christian Counseling

Heal · Counsel · Transform

B.A. in Human Services with a Concentration in Christian Counseling.

  • Christian counseling theory
  • Crisis & trauma intervention
