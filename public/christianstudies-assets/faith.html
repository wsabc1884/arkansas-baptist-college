A Formation.

The James & Rosa McKissic School of Christian Studies doesn't separate academic life from spiritual life — because we don't believe they should be separated. Every course, every chapel service, and every community experience is designed to form the whole person: intellect, character, and calling.

We belong to the Baptist tradition that has anchored African American faith communities for generations. That heritage is not just history — it's the living water this program draws from every day.

Hands-On Ministry

Ministry Opportunities

You graduate having already led. Ministry starts here — not after graduation.

⛪
Church Partnership Placements

Serve in real ministry roles at partner churches across Little Rock and the surrounding region — preaching, teaching, leading worship, or assisting with administration.

🏥
Chaplaincy & Pastoral Care

Clinical pastoral experience through hospital and hospice chaplaincy partnerships — learning to minister in the hardest and holiest moments of human life.

👶
Youth & Children's Ministry

Lead youth groups, children's programs, and vacation Bible school in partnership with local churches. Build the next generation from the very beginning.

🎤
Preaching & Pulpit Practice

Regular opportunities to preach in chapel, at partner churches, and in the Homiletics Lab — with video recording, faculty review, and external preacher critique.

Community Service

Engage Little Rock's communities through youth intervention programs, non-profit partnerships, and social justice initiatives rooted in the prophetic Black church tradition.

🎵
Worship Leadership Lab

Hands-on worship leading experience in chapel, church services, and the Worship Arts Lab — developing musical excellence alongside liturgical theology.

Spiritual Formation Pathway

Called. Equipped. Sent.

Three movements of Christian formation. The arc of every ABC Christian Studies graduate.

1

Called

You arrive at ABC already sensing a pull toward something greater. The first year is about discovering, naming, and articulating that calling — with faculty who've walked the same road. Biblical foundations, church history, and spiritual formation ground you in who you are and where you come from.

2

Equipped

Years two and three go deep into your concentration. Whether it's preaching, administration, worship, or education — you develop the skills, the theology, and the character that ministry actually demands. You're not just learning about leadership; you're practicing it in real ministry contexts.

3

Sent

You finish with a capstone project reviewed by external ministry practitioners, a personal ministry philosophy, and the community and credentials to step into your calling with confidence. You leave ABC not looking for your calling — you leave living it.
